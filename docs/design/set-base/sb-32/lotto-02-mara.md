## 1. Lazo del Rider

`base.mara.CinghiataDiTraverso`

~~~text
Create one new square illustration, 1024 x 1024 pixels or larger, aspect ratio 1:1, opaque, preferably an original PNG: the action artwork for a playing card of the original project Botte Micidiali. Full-bleed artwork only, not a card mockup: no frame, no border, no lettering.

Attachment 1 is the approved character reference: draw the same woman with the same face and body. Do not copy its pose, framing or plain background. Attachment 2 is the approved interior of the same dive bar: show its shelves, bottles and lamp visibly behind the figures, simplified, darker and less sharp than the subject.

Style: hand-inked European comic, irregular dark contours, restrained painted shading, light worn print texture, slightly exaggerated features. Restrained palette: walnut and tobacco browns, dusty bottle green, oxblood burgundy, muted petrol, worn ivory; no oversaturated colours. Warm, dirty light from one clear direction, deep shadows that never become flat black.

Composition for a tiny card tile: one centred subject filling at least two thirds of the image height, with a large clear silhouette readable at 60 pixels. Keep the background darker, simpler and less sharp than the subject, with strong value contrast. Keep essential details out of the outer 5% of the image. Give characters and bar furniture a believable relative scale, and make any seat physically plausible for the character's weight. Cast shadows consistently with the direction of the light. Show a single clear, coherent action in motion with physical force and impact, not a posed figure. Make the anatomy of arms and hands coherent and their ownership unambiguous.

Subject: Mara snaps a loose strap of her delivery backpack across the torso of a shadowy opponent; the strap is taut at impact and the opponent recoils.

Mara has long black hair and headphones; she wears a reflective sea-green vest over a dark hoodie and a cubic delivery backpack. She holds her foil helmet in one hand, never on her head. Any opponent is a generic shadowy figure without a recognizable face.

Avoid: mirroring the character; changing her face; helmet on her head; delivery service brands; text, letters, numbers, pseudo-lettering, signatures, watermarks, logos or brands, including on clothing and bottles; recognizable faces on opponents; other cast members; crowds; blood; card frames, borders or interface; likeness of any real person; photographic or 3D-rendered style; oversaturated colours, dominant yellow-and-black, neon, gold ornament; Internet imagery or the look of any existing game.
~~~
