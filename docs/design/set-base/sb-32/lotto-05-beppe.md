I blocchi seguenti sono i testi letterali da inviare, senza modifiche. Dieci carte (Beppe non ha un’ultima risorsa); identificatori dal file del set, congelati da [D213](../../../set-base-decisioni.md#decisioni-del-proprietario). Soggetti dalle note d’illustrazione della [griglia](../griglia.md). La parte comune è quella del lotto 3 con una riga in più dalle correzioni del lotto 4 ([D214](../../../set-base-decisioni.md#decisioni-del-proprietario) (2)): ogni attrezzo e oggetto ha una forma unica e coerente, impugnata in modo naturale. Dalla guida di stile: tasche senza attrezzi in vista, salvo l’attrezzo che la carta mostra in uso. Scelte della regia: in Disassemblare l’oggetto smontato è uno shaker da cocktail; Secondo Bagagliaio è all’aperto, davanti al bar, con un’auto senza marchio né targa; in Chiave da Tredici nessuna ferita visibile.

## 1. Questo Torna Utile

`base.gigi.QuestoTornaUtile`

~~~text
Create one new square illustration, 1024 x 1024 pixels or larger, aspect ratio 1:1, opaque, preferably an original PNG: the action artwork for a playing card of the original project Botte Micidiali. Full-bleed artwork only, not a card mockup: no frame, no border, no lettering.

Attachment 1 is the approved character reference: draw the same man with the same face and body. Do not copy its pose, framing or plain background. Attachment 2 is the approved interior of the same dive bar: show its shelves, bottles and lamp visibly behind the figures, simplified, darker and less sharp than the subject.

Style: hand-inked European comic, irregular dark contours, restrained painted shading, light worn print texture, slightly exaggerated features. Restrained palette: walnut and tobacco browns, dusty bottle green, oxblood burgundy, muted petrol, worn ivory; no oversaturated colours. Warm, dirty light from one clear direction, deep shadows that never become flat black.

Composition for a tiny card tile: one centred subject filling at least two thirds of the image height, with a large clear silhouette readable at 60 pixels. Keep the background darker, simpler and less sharp than the subject, with strong value contrast. Keep essential details out of the outer 5% of the image. Give characters and bar furniture a believable relative scale, and make any seat physically plausible for the character's weight. Cast shadows consistently with the direction of the light. Show a single clear, coherent action in motion with physical force and impact, not a posed figure. Make the anatomy of arms and hands coherent and their ownership unambiguous: every person has exactly two arms and two hands, and no extra or detached limb appears anywhere. Draw every tool and object as one coherent, physically plausible object with a single recognizable shape, no doubled, fused or impossible parts, held by a hand that grips it naturally.

Subject: Beppe crouches by a smashed bar chair and lifts one of its broken wooden legs, weighing it in his hand with the satisfied frown of a connoisseur, already turning it to inspect the joint; the rest of the chair lies in pieces at his feet.

Beppe is sturdy and planted, about forty-five, with a long thick beard and long hair gathered in a bun on top of his head. He wears tobacco-brown canvas work overalls with both straps up, shiny with grease at the knees, over a black T-shirt of an invented extreme metal band with an illegible logo made of thorns and roots; another illegible thorny logo is tattooed on his forearm. His pockets are empty: the only tools in sight are those the subject names, if any. Any opponent or patron is a generic shadowy figure without a recognizable face, anonymous through shadow and the turn of the head; no hood on any visible figure.

Avoid: mirroring the character; changing his face; tools sticking out of his pockets; tools other than those the subject names; a readable band name or the logo of any real band; hoods on any figure; text, letters, numbers, pseudo-lettering, signatures, watermarks, labels, logos or brands, including on clothing, tools, bottles; recognizable faces on opponents; other cast members; crowds; blood; card frames, borders or interface; likeness of any real person; photographic or 3D-rendered style; oversaturated colours, dominant yellow-and-black, neon, gold ornament; Internet imagery or the look of any existing game.
~~~
