# SB-32 — Ultime correzioni a Piera e Grappino

Il proprietario chiede di proporzionare meglio la seduta nell'immagine principale di Piera, conservare la seduta della carta Seltz, allineare il getto al beccuccio e correggere la zampa di Grappino. Ogni blocco usa il solo allegato indicato ed è committato prima della generazione.

## Piera — personaggio

Allegato `originali/base.character.Pino-5.png`.

~~~text
Edit the attached square illustration. Keep Piera, her face, body, pose, black clothing, glass, pencil in her hair, dog, bar, lighting and composition unchanged. Correct the proportions of her backless seat: replace the overly broad bench-like structure with a modest, realistically sized, sturdy dark wooden bar stool with a single round padded seat, level with her hips and mostly hidden behind her body. Its legs are straight, properly spaced, grounded and support her naturally; no backrest. The stool remains understated in the dark bar palette. Preserve the hand-inked European comic style.

Avoid: an oversized or bench-like seat, backrest or unstable geometry; changing Piera, the dog or the setting; text, letters, numbers, pseudo-lettering, signatures, watermarks, logos or brands; card frames, borders or interface; photographic or 3D-rendered style.
~~~
